The Club

More than a team. A path forward.

The Valkyries Basketball Association is a women's and girls' basketball club based in Antsirabe, in Madagascar's Vakinankaratra highlands. Born from the Solidarity Club community and powered by Skyline NGO, the club uses sport to keep girls in school, build confidence, and open doors that sport, education and travel can unlock.

Every practice, jersey and away game is made possible by supporters around the world. Your gift goes straight to the court — and to the girls who live for it.
